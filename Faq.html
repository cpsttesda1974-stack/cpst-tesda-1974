Frequently Asked Questions

What are the official school hours?

We are open at Monday to Friday / 8am to 5pm

What are the requirements?

📌 Certified true copy of Official Transcript of record (TOR)
📌 Certified true copy of Form 137 (Permanent Record if the candidates previous education is Elementary or Secondary Level)
📌 PSA birth certificate (xerox copy)
📌 1x1 ID picture with name tag, with collar and white background (studio type)
📌 Passport size ID picture with name tag, with collar and white background (studio type)


Are we allowed to park inside the CPST compound?

Yes, we have designated parking inside (best for motorcycles), but slots can fill up quickly!

Where CPST located?

Central Pangasinan School of Technology is located at Montemayor St. Brgy. Poblacion Malasiqui, Philippines

Is the Enrollment ongoing?

Since our enrollment is ongoing and we have walk-ins coming in, just let the staff know at the gate so they can assist you.
